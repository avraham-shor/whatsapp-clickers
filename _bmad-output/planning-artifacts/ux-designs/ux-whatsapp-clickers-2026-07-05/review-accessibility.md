# Accessibility Review — whatsapp-clickers

Reviewer lens: accessibility (WCAG 2.1 AA floor + real-user impact per SM-5 — three generations answer without assistance).
Reviewed: `DESIGN.md`, `EXPERIENCE.md` (spine pair), `.decision-log.md` (A1–A14 confirmed), PRD 2026-07-08 (FR-1..FR-18, SM-1..SM-6).
All contrast ratios below were recomputed independently (WCAG relative-luminance formula), not taken from the spine.

## Overall verdict

The accessibility floor is unusually deliberate for a planning-stage spine — the headline 7.1:1 claim is mathematically correct, color is never the sole reveal signal, reduced-motion is enumerated per animation, and non-answerer silence is genuinely humane design. However, a token collision (`success` == `green-600`, both `#16A34A`) silently voids the claimed distribution-bar distinction; the Reveal state — the game's most important moment — carries the weakest text contrast on the stage (3.3:1 before projector washout); and the projection type ramp is specified in device pixels with no viewing-geometry basis, so its "sufficient at 5–15m" premise is unverifiable and likely false in typical family-event rooms. None of this blocks Finalize, but the five high findings should be resolved in-spine before stories are cut for §4.2/§4.3.

## Findings

- **[high]** Token collision: `success: '#16A34A'` and `green-600: '#16A34A'` are the same color (DESIGN.md frontmatter `colors:`). The `distribution-bar` spec — "Fill {colors.green-600}; the correct option's bar fills {colors.success}" — therefore renders zero visual difference between the correct bar and every other bar, for all viewers, not only CVD viewers. It also falsifies DESIGN.md §Colors' claim that "Semantic colors … are separate from the brand green." *Fix:* re-point the `success` token to a distinct, darker value (e.g., `#15803D` — white text on it = 5.0:1, AA for normal text) **and**, independently of color, mark the correct bar with the same ✓ used on `stage-option-correct` (in its count/percent label) — two greens will never be reliably distinguishable for deutan/protan viewers at distance, so the bar needs the non-color marker regardless.

- **[high]** Reveal is the lowest-contrast moment on the stage. `stage-option-correct` puts `ink-on-dark` white text and the ✓ icon on `success` `#16A34A` = **3.30:1** (DESIGN.md frontmatter `stage-option-correct`; EXPERIENCE.md §Audience Display stages, Reveal). That passes AA only via the large-text/non-text 3:1 allowance — and DESIGN.md §Elevation itself acknowledges projectors wash content out. A daylight-washed projector will push the single most important piece of information in the game (which answer was right) below effective legibility, exactly when the whole room looks up. Compare 7.13:1 on ordinary un-revealed options: the hierarchy is inverted. *Fix:* the darker `success` value from the previous finding (`#15803D` → 5.0:1 with white) fixes both findings with one token change; keep the ✓ icon and the dimming of wrong options as the redundant channels they already are.

- **[high]** The type ramp has no viewing-geometry basis (DESIGN.md §Typography "Projection scale", A9: timer 96px, question 64px, options/leaderboard 40px, floor 32px at 1080p). Pixels say nothing about legibility; projected physical size does. Rule of thumb: comfortable reading needs letter height ≈ distance/250. On a typical family-event projection ~2.2m wide (100"), 1px ≈ 1.15mm, so 40px type ≈ 30mm Hebrew glyph height — comfortable to ~7m, marginal at 10m, gone at 15m; the 32px counters/labels die around 6m. On a 65" TV (a realistic living-room fallback for a 30-person family event) everything halves. Question text is mitigated — every Participant holds a private copy in WhatsApp — but the **leaderboard, distribution bars, live answer count, and progress label exist only on the display**, and leaderboard names at 40px are exactly what grandparents at the back most want to read (UJ-5, SM-5). *Fix:* (a) state the design's viewing assumption explicitly in DESIGN.md ("ramp assumes projected image ≥2.5m wide; legible to ~12m") so it is testable at pilot events; (b) raise the display-only surfaces one step — leaderboard rows and distribution labels to 48px, nothing below 40px on stage — the current layouts have the room (10 rows × 72px min-height leaves headroom in a 984px content area); (c) add one line of Organizer guidance to the lobby/preflight: "stand at the back row and read the smallest text before starting."

- **[high]** The join step underserves the users SM-3/SM-5 measure. `stage-lobby` gives the JOIN code Display 900 at 120px+, but the **phone number** — which every participant must transcribe into a new contact before they can send anything, on a kosher phone with no browser and no wa.me link — lives in the ordinary Heading instruction line ("שלחו JOIN [קוד] למספר [מספר]", DESIGN.md frontmatter `stage-lobby`; EXPERIENCE.md Flow 1 step 3). Transcribing 10 digits from a projector is the hardest visual task in the entire product for low-vision and elderly participants, and it gates everything. Separately, that instruction line mixes RTL Hebrew with LTR tokens (JOIN, Latin code, phone digits) and neither spine mandates bidi isolation — without `<bdi>`/`dir` spans (and their plaintext equivalents in WhatsApp copy), the Unicode bidi algorithm can visually reorder code/number fragments. *Fix:* render the phone number at the same Display scale as the JOIN code, grouped and in `tabular-nums` (e.g., 050-123-4567); add a spine rule that JOIN codes and phone numbers are always bidi-isolated on web surfaces and composed bidi-safe in `messages_he.go` templates; add Organizer guidance to pre-share the number as a contact card in the family WhatsApp group (out-of-band, zero product cost, removes the transcription entirely for most of the room).

- **[high]** Host keyboard shortcuts as specified will misfire at live-event stakes (EXPERIENCE.md §Interaction Primitives: "Space = primary CTA action. Escape = confirm-stop dialog"). Two conflicts: (1) Space natively activates the *focused* element — if the Organizer last clicked/tabbed to "סיים משחק" (a visible secondary CTA in two states of the control table), a global Space handler and native activation now race, and the failure mode is ending the game in front of 80 people; (2) using Escape to *open* a dialog inverts its universal meaning (dismiss) — the second Escape press inside the confirm-stop dialog becomes ambiguous, and screen-reader users are trained on Esc-closes. The spine already flags the list as unconfirmed; this is the confirmation it needs. *Fix:* Space triggers the primary CTA only when focus is on `body`/the main region and never inside inputs or dialogs; when a button has focus, native behavior wins (no global handler); replace Escape-opens-dialog with a dedicated visible "עצור" control (Escape then keeps its one meaning: close the dialog); keep destructive advances ("סיים משחק") behind the confirm dialog in all paths, keyboard included.

- **[medium]** Digits 1–4 are accepted but never advertised. FR-5 and the conversation grammar accept "Letter א–ד / digit 1–4" (EXPERIENCE.md §WhatsApp conversation grammar), yet the MCQ template says only "השיבו באות אחת (א–ד)" and the format hint only "שלחו אות אחת: א, ב, ג או ד" (§WhatsApp message templates). Digits are the easier motor path (numeric row, one tap, no Hebrew-keyboard state) for tremor, low literacy, children, and screen-reader users — the exact populations SM-5 watches — and the affordance is hidden. *Fix:* one copy change in two templates: "השיבו באות (א–ד) או בספרה (1–4)". Keep the options themselves lettered only — א=1 order is self-evident in Hebrew.

- **[medium]** Nothing tells Participants to stay in the chat. An elderly participant who locks their phone or drifts to the family group chat spends 5–10 seconds per question unlocking and re-finding the conversation — against a 20s default window (A8) that's a consistent structural miss, and the failure is invisible (non-answerers get silence). The Welcome template says "עיניים למסך הגדול" but not what to do with the phone (EXPERIENCE.md §WhatsApp message templates, Welcome). *Fix:* one clause in Welcome: "השאירו את הצ'אט הזה פתוח — השאלות יגיעו לכאן". Optionally repeat in the stage-lobby instruction line.

- **[medium]** Dimmed wrong options fall to ~2.1:1. `stage-option-dimmed` keeps green-800 at opacity 0.45 (DESIGN.md frontmatter); composited over the white body, white option text lands at ≈2.1:1 — effectively unreadable at distance. But the Reveal is exactly when the room needs to *re-read* the wrong options: the distribution bars invite "רוב החדר אמר ג!" (Flow 5), which is meaningless if nobody can still read what ג said, and low-vision participants can't verify what they answered. *Fix:* dim the fill, not the text — e.g., background opacity 0.55–0.6 with the option letter/text held at full `ink-on-dark` (yields ≥4.5:1), or swap dimmed rows to `green-100` background with `text-primary` text. Distinction from the correct row is already carried by the ✓ and the success fill; the dim only needs to demote, not erase.

- **[medium]** `timer-inline` fails non-text contrast and breaks the gold rule. Its permanent "3px border {colors.gold}" sits on white/`host-surface` at ≈**1.6:1** (WCAG 1.4.11 needs 3:1) — a low-vision Organizer can't see their own mirror of the authoritative countdown (DESIGN.md frontmatter `timer-inline`). It also contradicts DESIGN.md §Brand & Style: "Gold appears in exactly two moments" — this is a third, permanent one. *Fix:* ring in `green-800` (6.9:1 on host surfaces); at ≤5s switch ring/numeral to `error` `#DC2626` (4.8:1 on white — semantically "urgent" on the tool surface, preserving gold's stage-only meaning). Add the general token rule this exposes: gold is only ever used on `green-800`/`green-900` surfaces, never on light ones.

- **[medium]** Live regions as specified will flood screen readers. The Accessibility Floor mandates `aria-live="polite"` on counters (EXPERIENCE.md §Accessibility Floor, Semantics) — but the answer counter ("63 ענו") updates up to ~80 times in 20 seconds and the lobby counter climbs continuously; a screen-reader user who opens the display page gets an unbroken announcement queue that also talks over the assertive stage transitions. The per-second timer numeral's live-region status is unspecified. *Fix:* specify throttling — announce counters at most every 5s or at milestones ("50 ענו"); explicitly exclude the timer numeral from any live region, announcing only question-open ("20 שניות") and the ≤5s threshold; keep `assertive` for stage transitions only.

- **[medium]** The 200%-zoom claim contradicts the responsive floor. §Accessibility Floor promises the Host Dashboard "functional and non-overlapping at 200% zoom (WCAG 1.4.4)", but §Responsive & Platform sets minimum supported width at 1024px and calls the 768px single-column collapse "degraded and untargeted." 200% zoom on a 1280px laptop is a 640px effective viewport — squarely in the territory the spine disowns. *Fix:* promote the single-column collapse from "untargeted" to a supported, tested mode for the builder and live-control surfaces (it is the same code path zoom hits); the live-control panel is a natural single column anyway (one primary CTA + stats).

- **[medium]** Blind participants have no time-remaining channel at all. The limit is stated once, at the *end* of the question message — after TalkBack has read the question and four options — and the only countdown lives on a screen they cannot see; per-second WhatsApp ticks are (rightly) rejected (EXPERIENCE.md §Inspiration & Anti-patterns). With the 20s default (A8), the SR round-trip (listen ~10–15s, switch to composer, type, send) barely fits or doesn't. The graceful closed-question reply and non-answerer silence soften the miss, but SM-5's "without assistance" quietly fails for this group. *Fix:* no new machinery — (a) surface the per-question time limit prominently in the builder with guidance that 30–45s accommodates screen-reader and slower-motor participants (Organizer-paced closing already exists; the timer is not auto-advance); (b) note in the spine that the room's audible last-five-seconds count (Flow 5) is the de-facto non-visual urgency cue, making it a designed behavior rather than luck.

- **[low]** Focus is orphaned on host state changes. When the primary CTA swaps ("פתח שאלה" → "סגור שאלה", §Host control panel), a replaced DOM node drops keyboard focus to `body`, forcing re-tabbing mid-game and leaving SR users unaware the state advanced. *Fix:* one persistent primary-action button whose label and action swap in place (focus retained), plus a polite live-region announcement of the new state.

- **[low]** Distribution-bar label placement is ambiguous. "Count + percent label in {colors.text-secondary}" (DESIGN.md frontmatter `distribution-bar`) is 7.5:1 on the white body but **2.27:1 if overlaid on the green-600 fill**. *Fix:* specify labels always render outside the fill (inline-end of the bar track, on white).

- **[low]** Reduced-motion for the room is gated on the Organizer's OS preference. The audience — including vestibular-sensitive viewers — cannot set `prefers-reduced-motion` on a projector (§Accessibility Floor, Motion). Current animations are mild (400ms bars, ≤300ms fades, falling confetti; no flashing), so impact is low. *Fix:* a single "הפחת אנימציות" toggle in the dashboard's display controls that forces the already-specified static equivalents.

- **[low]** The ≤5s urgency cue is a hue-only change (white ring → gold, `timer-hero`). The counting numeral is the redundant channel, so 1.4.1 is technically met, but from 15m a hue shift on a thin ring is weak for low-vision and CVD viewers. *Fix:* pair the gold shift with a non-color change that survives reduced-motion — thicken the ring (8px → 14px) at ≤5s.

- **[low]** Two stage details escape the specs: the leaderboard reshuffle animation (Flow 5 "מתערבב") is not explicitly named in the reduced-motion list (only "transitions" — make it explicit), and the ▲ mover indicator (A11) has no size spec — it must obey the ≥32px stage floor or it becomes decorative noise the back rows can't parse.

- **[low]** Welcome's rename hint stacks colons: "(לא [שם]? שלחו: שם: השם שלכם)" — awkward for TalkBack (two announced colons) and cognitively noisy for the exact users who need renaming least friction. *Fix:* "(לא [שם]? שלחו לדוגמה — שם: רחל לוי)".

## What the spine already does well

- The headline contrast claim is *true*: white on `green-800` = 7.13:1, recomputed and verified (DESIGN.md §Accessibility floor cross-ref).
- Color is never the sole signal at Reveal — ✓/✗ icons mandated alongside fills (DESIGN.md Do's and Don'ts) — and Kahoot's color-coded option grid was explicitly rejected for cognitive-load reasons.
- Dual answer format (א–ד **and** 1–4) exists at the intake layer — the fix above is copy-only.
- Non-answerer silence (FR-6) and the polite closed-question reply are dignity-preserving by design; nobody is scolded in front of family.
- Universal Reply (FR-2): no inbound message — media, stickers, gibberish — ever dead-ends; this is a real cognitive-accessibility guarantee.
- System-font stack with zero external requests: Hebrew renders correctly behind every content filter, on every device.
- WhatsApp copy is plain-text, front-loaded, digit-based, and emoji-disciplined (A1: symbolic set, max one per message) — near-ideal for TalkBack/VoiceOver linear reading.
- Reduced-motion coverage is enumerated per animation with defined static equivalents (timer, bars, confetti, transitions), not hand-waved.
- No auto-advance anywhere: Organizer pacing (rejected auto-advance, §Inspiration & Anti-patterns) is itself the product's strongest accommodation — a slow room gets the time it needs.
- The whole ramp is in rem; `dir="rtl"` + `lang="he"` mandated on every document; focus rings preserved; errors via `aria-describedby`, never orphan toasts.
- Gender-neutral Hebrew address (A2) — inclusive by construction, not by exception.
