WhatsApp Clickers — UX Design Validation Report

_bmad-output/planning-artifacts/ux-designs/ux-whatsapp-clickers-2026-07-05/ (OQ-7 revision, 2026-07-09)

A strong, source-extractable spine pair: all five PRD journeys are re-narrated as Key Flows with climaxes, every {token} reference in both files resolves, the conversation grammar (state × message-class) is genuinely contract-grade, and the two-palette / gold-discipline system is coherent end to end. One high-severity gap blocks clean downstream extraction — the Audience Display has no defined content for Free-Text Questions (question stage and reveal stage), which epics Stories 4.3/4.4 explicitly wait on. Beyond that, the issues are a cluster of copy-contract self-inconsistencies (emoji rule vs. templates, an unimplementable placeholder) and cross-document ripples (20s vs. 30s default, UX-DR15 supersession) that are cheap to fix but would confuse an AI story-dev if left standing.

The accessibility lens materially sharpens the picture: a token collision (success == green-600, both #16A34A) silently voids the distribution-bar's claimed correct-answer distinction, and puts the game's most important moment — the Reveal — at the stage's weakest text contrast (3.3:1 before projector washout). The projection type ramp is specified in device pixels with no viewing-geometry basis, the lobby undersizes the hardest visual task in the product (transcribing the phone number), and the host keyboard shortcuts as specified can end a game by accident. None of this blocks Finalize; the six combined highs are all resolvable in-spine with token-level and copy-level fixes.

Accessibility review

review-accessibility.md

The accessibility floor is unusually deliberate for a planning-stage spine — the headline 7.1:1 claim is mathematically correct, color is never the sole reveal signal, reduced-motion is enumerated per animation, and non-answerer silence is genuinely humane design. However, a token collision silently voids the claimed distribution-bar distinction; the Reveal — the game's most important moment — carries the weakest text contrast on the stage; and the projection type ramp's "sufficient at 5–15m" premise is unverifiable and likely false in typical family-event rooms. All contrast ratios recomputed independently. 0 critical / 5 high / 7 medium / 5 low.

high

Token collision: success == green-600 (#16A34A) — the correct distribution bar cannot render as distinct

DESIGN.md · frontmatter colors
Zero visual difference between the correct bar and every other bar, for all viewers; also falsifies "semantic colors are separate from brand green."
Fix: Re-point success to #15803D (white = 5.0:1) and mark the correct bar with ✓ in its label — two greens are never distinguishable for deutan/protan viewers at distance.
high

Reveal is the lowest-contrast moment on the stage (white on success = 3.30:1)

DESIGN.md · stage-option-correct
Passes AA only via the large-text allowance; projector washout pushes the game's most important information below effective legibility. Hierarchy inverted vs. 7.13:1 on un-revealed options.
Fix: The same success → #15803D token change resolves both findings.
high

Type ramp has no viewing-geometry basis; display-only surfaces go illegible past ~6–7m

DESIGN.md · Typography (A9)
Question text is mitigated (private copy in WhatsApp) but leaderboard, distribution bars, counters exist only on the display — and 40px names are what the back row most wants to read (UJ-5, SM-5).
Fix: State the viewing assumption; raise display-only surfaces one step (leaderboard/distribution labels 48px, stage floor 40px); add back-row preflight guidance.
high

Lobby undersizes the phone number — the hardest visual task in the product — and bidi isolation is unmandated

DESIGN.md · stage-lobby; EXPERIENCE.md · Flow 1
Every participant must transcribe 10 digits from the projector (kosher phones: no wa.me links); mixed RTL/LTR content can visually reorder without bidi isolation.
Fix: Phone number at Display scale, grouped, tabular-nums; spine rule for bidi isolation on web + messages_he.go; Organizer guidance to pre-share the number as a contact card.
high

Host keyboard shortcuts will misfire at live-event stakes

EXPERIENCE.md · Interaction Primitives
Global Space races native focused-button activation (failure mode: ending the game in front of 80 people); Escape-opens-dialog inverts its universal meaning.
Fix: Space fires primary CTA only with focus on body/main; native behavior wins on focused buttons; dedicated visible "עצור" control; Escape only closes; destructive advances always behind the confirm dialog.
medium

Digits 1–4 accepted but never advertised

EXPERIENCE.md · templates
Digits are the easier motor path for tremor, low literacy, children, and screen-reader users — exactly SM-5's populations.
Fix: "השיבו באות (א–ד) או בספרה (1–4)" in the question template and format hint.
medium

Nothing tells Participants to stay in the chat

EXPERIENCE.md · Welcome template
Locking the phone or drifting to another chat costs 5–10s per question against a 20s window; the failure is invisible (non-answerers get silence).
Fix: One clause in Welcome: "השאירו את הצ'אט הזה פתוח — השאלות יגיעו לכאן."
medium

Dimmed wrong options fall to ~2.1:1 — unreadable exactly when the room re-reads them

DESIGN.md · stage-option-dimmed
The distribution bars invite "רוב החדר אמר ג!" — meaningless if nobody can still read what ג said.
Fix: Dim the fill, not the text — e.g., green-100 background with text-primary text; the ✓ and success fill already carry the distinction.
medium

timer-inline fails non-text contrast (gold on white ≈ 1.6:1) and breaks the two-moment gold rule

DESIGN.md · timer-inline
Fix: Ring in green-800; at ≤5s switch to error red (urgent on the tool surface); add rule: gold only ever on green-800/green-900 surfaces.
medium
